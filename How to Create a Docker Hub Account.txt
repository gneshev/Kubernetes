
Step 1: Go to the Docker Hub Sign-Up Page
Open your web browser and navigate to hub.docker.com/signup.

Step 2: Choose Your Sign-Up Method
You can create an account in one of two ways:

You can sign up using a direct email address or sign in with a third-party account (Google or GitHub):

Email: Enter a valid email address and set a password (must be at least 9 characters long).

Google / GitHub: Select your provider and authorize Docker to link your existing account.

Step 3: Set Your Docker ID (Username)
Enter a unique username. This is your permanent Docker ID.

Important Details:

Enter a unique username. This will serve as your permanent Docker ID for your profile and repository paths (e.g., docker.io/your-id/repository-name).

Requirements: 4 to 30 characters long, containing only numbers and lowercase letters.

Note: You cannot change or reuse your Docker ID once created.

Step 4: Accept Terms and Complete Registration
Review and check the box to accept Docker's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then click Sign Up.

Step 5: Verify Your Email
Check your email inbox for a confirmation message from Docker.

If registering via email, copy the One-Time Password (OTP) code sent to you and enter it on the Docker verification page.

If registering via Google or GitHub, ensure that your provider email is fully verified.

Step 6: Log In and Access Your Dashboard
Go back to hub.docker.com.

Log in using your Docker ID (or email address) and password.
